# Project Language

## Canonical FQCNN

The frozen ten-qubit, fully unitary model whose architecture identity is protected by the repository's regression evidence. Experimental variants never silently replace it.

## Core evidence set

The smallest coherent body of theory, experiments, comparisons, and reproducibility evidence required to support the submitted paper's actual claims.

## Respectable journal submission

A technically honest, reproducible paper with a clear contribution, repeated controlled experiments, relevant baselines, statistical uncertainty, explicit limitations, and no claim that depends on unfinished work. It does not imply a particular journal quartile or guaranteed acceptance.

## Q1-targeted journal submission

A respectable journal submission deliberately strengthened for top-quartile review through multi-domain evidence, repeated-seed comparisons, classical and quantum baselines, ablation evidence, local noise and resource analysis, reproducibility, and conservative claims. It is a quality target, not a guarantee of ranking, review outcome, or acceptance.

## Submission-ready

The manuscript and supplement agree with validated evidence, build successfully, disclose their scope and limitations, and contain no known unsupported numerical or novelty claim.

## Deferred study

Work intentionally excluded from the core evidence set. It may be described only as a limitation or future direction, never as completed evidence.

## Smoke evidence

A small execution used to verify that a path works. Smoke evidence cannot support a headline scientific result.

## Registered task

A predeclared dataset-and-class-pair combination with checksummed source files, source-stable sample IDs, and a split identity that hashes those IDs. Registered task names include the dataset family, so equal class numbers across families cannot collide.

## Scientific evidence

A completed, provenance-valid run produced under the frozen comparison protocol. It is stored separately from smoke runs and may enter manuscript aggregation only after artifact validation.
