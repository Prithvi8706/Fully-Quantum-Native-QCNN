# Author declarations, in plain language

This checklist is the part the software cannot complete for you. It is not a
request for another experiment: it records author, institutional, legal, and
publication facts that are not inferable from the code. Every listed author
should review the final PDF and the answers below before a journal portal is
used.

**Listed authors in the current draft:** Aasa Singh Bhui and Prithvi Raghu.
Names, affiliations, student status, and email addresses were transcribed from
the historical conference PDF; the authors must confirm that they remain current.

## What must be confirmed

- **Authorship and approval:** every listed author made a qualifying contribution,
  agrees with the author order, and approved the exact submitted version.
- **Contributions:** record the roles (for example, conceptualisation, theory,
  software, experiments, analysis, writing, and supervision) using the venue's
  required CRediT format if requested.
- **Originality and prior version:** the work is not under review elsewhere, and
  the earlier conference draft is disclosed as described in
  `conference-extension.md`.
- **Funding:** list grants, institutional support, and donated computing, or state
  that the authors confirmed that no specific funding applies.
- **Competing interests:** each author has supplied a current conflict statement.
  Employment or institutional affiliations should be disclosed exactly as the
  selected venue requires; no conflict is inferred by this repository.
- **Ethics and data rights:** confirm that the study uses public benchmark datasets,
  recruits no participants, collects no new personal data, and complies with the
  upstream dataset licences and citations. If the target venue requires an ethics
  determination, obtain it from the institution rather than guessing.
- **Code and material rights:** confirm that the included source, figures, and
  third-party material can be redistributed under their stated licences.
- **AI/tool disclosure:** check the selected venue's policy and disclose any
  substantive generative-AI assistance if required. Authors remain responsible
  for verifying every scientific statement and code path.
- **Correspondence:** confirm the corresponding author, email, and ORCID values
  in the portal.
- **Licence and APC:** select the publication licence and confirm APC funding,
  waiver, or institutional-agreement status.

```text
Corresponding author:

Author contribution statement:
Funding statement:

```

These fields are deliberately blank. They must be completed from author and
institutional records; replacing them with a guessed "none" statement would be
unsafe and could make the submission inaccurate.
