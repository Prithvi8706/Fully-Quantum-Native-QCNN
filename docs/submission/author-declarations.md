# Author and publication declarations

Complete and sign this checklist with all authors immediately before submission.
The repository cannot make these personal or institutional declarations for them.

- **Authorship:** contribution roles satisfy the target journal's authorship policy;
  every author approved the submitted version.
- **Originality:** the manuscript is not simultaneously under review elsewhere;
  any preprint, thesis, conference, or earlier draft is disclosed to the editor.
- **Conflicts:** each author has supplied a current competing-interest statement,
  including employment-related disclosure where applicable.
- **Funding:** all grants, institutional support, and in-kind computing support are
  listed, or the authors have confirmed that none apply.
- **Data rights:** upstream dataset licences and citations are respected; the
  repository does not redistribute restricted source archives.
- **Code rights:** all included source and figures can be redistributed under the
  repository's stated licence, and third-party material is attributed.
- **AI/tool disclosure:** any substantive generative-AI assistance is disclosed
  according to the journal's policy; authors verified all scientific text and code.
- **Correspondence:** the corresponding author, ORCID (if required), and email have
  been confirmed in the portal.
- **APC and licence:** the authors chose the publication licence and confirmed APC,
  waiver, or institutional-agreement status.

Author names, signatures, dates, funding, conflicts, and correspondence are
deliberately not fabricated in this file.
