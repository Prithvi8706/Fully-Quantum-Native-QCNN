# Conference-to-journal extension disclosure

`FQCNN conference version (historical).pdf` is the tracked earlier conference
artifact. The current `fqcnn.tex`/`fqcnn.pdf` pair is the full IEEE journal draft.
The two files must not be treated as interchangeable result reports.

The journal version extends the earlier draft in the following concrete ways:

1. The frozen main path is stated and audited as global amplitude encoding with
   register-index gate support; the earlier local feature-map/correlation wording
   is not used for the headline model.
2. The coherent pooling construction is given an exact channel-equivalence theorem,
   a strict-containment proposition, and finite numerical controls.
3. The evaluation adds the predeclared four-task, three-family comparison with five
   seeds, same-split logistic/MLP/TTN baselines, uncertainty, and multiplicity-
   adjusted paired tests.
4. The journal package adds the finite pooling-transfer ablation, trainability and
   expressibility diagnostics, resource counts, local noise/fake-backend checks,
   provenance bindings, and clean-reproduction gate.
5. The expanded journal manuscript now exposes the completed secondary pooling-arm
   campaign, per-seed and paired-effect tables, information-dynamics audit,
   capacity/tape accounting, and a dedicated discussion of architectural,
   predictive, and scaling implications.
6. The journal manuscript reports the negative or inconclusive empirical findings
   and explicitly limits claims to simulator evidence; it does not claim quantum
   advantage, hardware performance, or universal trainability.

Suggested portal/editor wording (adapt to the chosen venue's policy):

> This manuscript substantially extends an earlier conference draft by adding a
> formal pooling analysis, a corrected multi-domain repeated-seed evaluation with
> matched manifests and baselines, additional bounded resource/noise evidence, and
> a reproducibility package. The earlier draft is disclosed as prior dissemination;
> the present manuscript's numerical claims are based on the new validated evidence
> package.
